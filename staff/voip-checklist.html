To prepare your free phone bill review and network readiness check, please send us:

1. A photo or PDF of your latest phone bill
2. A list of the phone numbers you want to keep
3. How many people need a phone, and whether they work at a desk, from home, or both
4. A photo of your internet router (and network cabinet, if you have one)
5. A time for a 30-minute remote session with someone at a computer in the office
6. The name of the person who can sign for the phone account
