VoIP is only as good as the network it runs on

Most call quality problems (echo, drop-outs, robotic voices) aren't caused by the VoIP provider. They're caused by the office network: an overloaded router, the wrong switch, no traffic priority for voice, or no backup power. That's the part we handle.

VoIP questions we get asked

Can we keep our existing phone number?

In most cases, yes. Your number is ported from your current provider to Switch Telecom. We coordinate the request and schedule the switch-over so calls aren't lost.

What happens to our phones during a power cut?

Without backup power, VoIP phones stop working when the router and switch lose power. We size and install a UPS to keep them running, and can set calls to divert to mobiles if the office goes offline completely.

How much internet speed do we need?

Less than most people expect. Each call uses a small, steady amount of bandwidth. Stability matters more than speed, which is why we check your connection and router before you switch.

Do we need new phones?

Not always. Some existing IP phones can be reused. If new handsets are needed, you buy them from us once-off, and we configure and install them.

Can we use our mobiles or laptops instead of desk phones?

Yes. Extensions can ring on a softphone app on a mobile or computer, as well as on desk phones.

Who do we call if something goes wrong?

Call us first. We'll work out whether it's the line or your network. Line and billing issues are handled with Switch Telecom; network, handset and user issues are handled by us.

What happens to the phone bill I send you?

We use it only to prepare your comparison. It goes directly to our team by email, is not stored on this website, and is deleted once we've sent you the result.

Is the bill review really free?

Yes. You'll get a one-page comparison with no obligation. If moving wouldn't save you money, we'll say so.

Is there a long-term contract?

Switch Telecom services run month to month after the initial term shown on your subscription form, with one calendar month's written notice to cancel. Handsets and network equipment are a once-off purchase.

Can we call emergency numbers on VoIP?

You shouldn't rely on VoIP for emergency calls to 112, 10111 or 10117. Calls may be delayed, and the call centre can't see where you are, so always give your address. Keep a mobile phone available for emergencies.
