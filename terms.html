Clients are responsible for providing a suitable physical environment for installed network equipment, including adequate power protection (UPS/backup power), and for managing user access privileges within their own systems.

Poseidon Network Systems acts as reseller and is responsible for the network, hardware, installation and user support described on our website. Where these terms and Switch Telecom's terms differ on the telecommunication service itself, Switch Telecom's terms apply.

Approval. Every VoIP order is subject to Switch Telecom's credit check and technical feasibility assessment, at Switch Telecom's discretion.

Term and cancellation. The initial term is set out in the Switch Telecom subscription form (30 days if none is specified). The service then renews month to month, and either party may cancel with at least one calendar month's written notice. Cancelling before the end of the initial term or a renewal period may result in a cancellation charge for the unexpired portion.

Billing. Switch Telecom invoices monthly: fixed fees in advance and call charges in arrears (or in advance on prepaid services), plus VAT, normally by debit order. Power and connectivity. VoIP services need a working internet connection and power at the router, switch and handset. They will not work during a power or internet outage unless backup power or call diversion is in place.

Emergency calls. VoIP services should not be relied on to reach emergency numbers (112, 10111, 10117). Calls may be delayed or routed to a distant call centre, and the call centre will not be able to see your location, so always tell the dispatcher where you are.

Unlimited packages. Switch Telecom's Unlimited VoIP Line packages are subject to fair-use terms. Unlimited VoIP Line Lite is for personal use only, and neither Lite nor Premium may be used with switchboards, gateways or trunking systems. Outbound call centres are excluded.
