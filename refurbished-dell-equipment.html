What comes with a refurbished Dell from Poseidon?

Where do you deliver?

Yes, within 40km of Cape Town, arranged per order. Further away, we can courier it at your cost. Please check your order and note any damage on the delivery note before you sign.

What warranty do I get?

Refurbished Dell units carry a 1-year warranty: if a unit is faulty, we repair or replace it. The battery is covered for 6 months. Accidental, liquid and power-surge damage aren’t covered.

How do I pay?

Payment is due before delivery. Businesses can pay within 30 days of invoice if agreed in writing.

Can I return it?

Yes, within set time limits. Businesses: tell us within 7 days of delivery if anything isn’t as ordered. Private buyers can cancel within 7 working days of delivery if the unit is unused and returned undamaged in its original packaging.
